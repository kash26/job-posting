# Product Requirements Document: Talent RDC Express

## 1. Project Overview
**Talent RDC Express** is a professional recruitment platform dedicated to connecting talent with top companies and global organizations in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). The platform aims to modernize the job market in Central Africa through a high-fidelity, tech-forward user interface inspired by modern tech academies.

## 2. Brand Identity & Design System
*   **Brand Name:** Talent RDC Express
*   **Primary Color:** Vibrant Blue (`#2563EB`)
*   **Typography:** Hanken Grotesk (Professional, modern, and highly readable)
*   **Logo:** A minimalist icon-only mark featuring a stylized person figure combined with a dynamic upward-pointing arrow, symbolizing career growth and potential.
*   **Visual Style:** 
    *   Professional tech aesthetic with subtle geometric background patterns.
    *   Rounded corners (md/lg) for a friendly yet structured feel.
    *   High contrast and generous whitespace for clarity and focus.

## 3. Core Features & User Flows
### Candidate Experience
*   **Smart Job Search:** Filterable search by job title, skills, or company with specific tags for Remote, Engineering, Finance, etc.
*   **Personal Dashboard:** Overview of applications, profile views, and interviews.
*   **Job Details:** High-information-density job cards with clear salary ranges, job types, and quick-apply actions.
*   **Professional Profiling:** Experience and education tracking with visual "Tech" styling.

### Future Scope (Planned)
*   **Employer Portal:** Interface for companies to post offers and manage talent pipelines.
*   **Premium "Talent RDC Pro":** Enhanced placement and direct access to recruiters.

## 4. Platform Architecture (Screens)
The platform is designed as a responsive web experience with dedicated optimizations for both Desktop and Mobile.

### Key Screens:
1.  **Home (Landing Page):** Hero section with search, sector browsing, and featured opportunities.
2.  **Job Listings (Offres):** List view with advanced filtering and pagination.
3.  **Job Detail (Poste):** Deep dive into requirements, overview, and company profile with fixed "Apply Now" CTA on mobile.
4.  **Candidate Dashboard:** Personal workspace for managing the recruitment journey.
5.  **Candidate Profile:** Public-facing or recruiter-facing view of talent assets.

## 5. Technical Specifications
*   **Framework:** HTML5 / Tailwind CSS
*   **Device Support:** Desktop (1440px+) and Mobile (390px portrait)
*   **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 compliant (in progress)
*   **Interactions:** Micro-interactions on buttons, hover states for job cards, and fixed navigation bars for mobile ergonomics.
