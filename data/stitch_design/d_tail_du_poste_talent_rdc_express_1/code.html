Job Overview +

Adobe is seeking a passionate Senior Product Designer to join our Creative Cloud team. You will be responsible for defining the user experience of next-generation design tools, working closely with researchers, product managers, and engineers to craft elegant solutions for millions of creative professionals.

To reach millions, we need more people like you: entrepreneurs, builders, and owners inside the company who are eager to grow at scale. Join us to empower more businesses and creatives with technology that feels magical yet intuitive.

What You Will Do

  • check_circle Design and iterate intuitive digital products that delight our users globally.
  • check_circle Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define sitemaps and user flows.
  • check_circle Uncover valuable insights through user research to inform design decisions.

Requirements

  • check_circle 5+ years of experience in UX/UI design for complex SaaS products.
  • check_circle Strong portfolio showcasing end-to-end product design process.
  • check_circle Expertise in Figma, Adobe XD, and interactive prototyping tools.

About Adobe

Adobe is an exciting new digital venture. Our proud purpose is to build a technology platform that will help make business possible for restaurant owners, hotel owners, and independent retailers.

Adobe PRO brings together the best talent across multi-nationals to transform the digital creative landscape through innovation and empathy.
